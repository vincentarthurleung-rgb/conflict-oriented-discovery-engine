# Granularity Bridge Policy v1

Status: Proposed

An exact canonical qualifier identity may deterministically yield `exact_match`. Any non-exact pair requires an explicit versioned mapping to yield `policy_equivalent` or `policy_compatible`. Without such a mapping it is `unresolved`, never inferred from names, string similarity, an LLM, or case-specific rules.

The initial policy is structural and contains no scientific mappings. Therefore it cannot establish compatibility or incompatibility for non-exact measurement levels, endpoint compartments, or endpoint subtypes.
