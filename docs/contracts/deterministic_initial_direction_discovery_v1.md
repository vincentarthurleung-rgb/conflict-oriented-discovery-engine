# Deterministic initial direction discovery v1

Owner scope: `runs/20261031_code_deterministic_conflict_discovery_recovery_offline/owner_instruction.original.txt`.
This is a derived exploratory output contract. No frozen scientific schema,
measurement ontology, threshold, E0–E2 freeze or formal L4 policy is changed.

`case_stages_v5` executes normalization/projection, initial sign discovery, then
optional AI review and optional Work comparison. Historical v1–v4 stage contracts
remain readable and immutable. Discovery reads **all retained native records**,
including records withheld by downstream inclusion/admission. Scientific paper
selection and existing extraction coverage are unchanged; absent extraction is
not evidence of absence.

The adapter reuses packet/source coordinate verification and native representation
verification. Raw V3, Core, intervention inventory, tested/reference arms, context,
evidence spans/hashes, extraction warnings and source revisions are retained.
Self-consistent span hashes alone are not independent scientific verification.

Binary entropy uses eligible +1/−1 votes only, excludes neutral/unknown/null,
requires both signs for a collision, and returns insufficient evidence when no
valid sign exists. A missing sign never defaults to +1. Nonsignificance and
source-reported absence of association are separate from established zero effect.
No causal sign is granted, including for observational associations.

Preliminary groups use exact extracted entity/endpoint/type and relationship
kind. These labels do not establish canonical identity or proposition equivalence.
No fuzzy matching, gene/protein alias invention, assay equivalence or new registry
classification is performed. Associations are partitioned from intervention or
exposure outcomes. Original contexts remain per observation.

* **RAW_POLARITY_COLLISION**: opposite eligible extracted signs in a preliminary
  typed group; orientation/alignment may remain unknown, including within-paper
  signals. This is not an assertion that the same proposition was tested.
* **PROVISIONAL_DIRECTIONAL_DISCORDANCE**: distinct recorded PMID and PMCID,
  exact factor and assay scope, exact same or reversed source-attested arms, and
  opposing signs after comparator orientation. Comparability remains unqualified.
  Different arms/dose or unresolved orientation permit only a raw collision.
* **QUALIFIED_CONTRADICTION_CANDIDATE**: only the existing qualification contracts;
  never issued by this lane.
* **FORMALLY_AUTHORIZED_CONFLICT**: only independent authorized scientific
  adjudication; never issued by this lane. Formal L4 remains staging_only.

A/B counts may overlap: one pair can have raw collision and provisional discordance.
Reversing comparator orientation can resolve a raw collision, or reveal a possible
discordance from equal raw signs. Entropy is a signal, not proof of contradiction.
Duplicate exact source/endpoint observations are collapsed independently of record
IDs. Different endpoints of one paper do not create independent publications;
publication-pair totals are unique across endpoint groups. Distinct publications
do not establish independent cohorts/laboratories.

Work/LLM comparison, modifier and hypothesis judgments remain source-bound
provisional review suggestions. They are not inputs to this algorithm. Their
absence, positive or negative verdicts do not alter its counts. Invalid source
evidence is retained as rejection/uncertainty, never repaired by model output.

Derived implementation hashes and exact native/source inputs bind discovery
outputs. The Pilot effective dependency manifest includes this implementation,
direction semantics and binary entropy. Raw provider-request cache identity and
paid request bytes are unchanged; old completed packages require a new revision.

Projection reports A/B separately from C/D and removes `true_conflicts_after`.
Legacy `conflict_candidates_after` continues to mean canonical polarity bundles,
not qualification or formal adjudication. C/D counts are zero because this lane
does not invoke those stages, not because the entire repository was adjudicated.
Dataset and Atlas preserve the full artifact alongside optional Work assessments.
