# Context Difference Authority v1

Difference artifact validity and new L4 authority are independent.
`authoritative` requires a ready Entry and a new-contract-valid Difference.
`ready_not_materialized` represents a ready Entry without such an artifact.
Historical valid artifacts may be `diagnostic_only`; they cannot be used
formally or enter L4b.

This contract produces no Comparability, Divergence Explanation, or formal
decision.
