# Historical Lineage Binding v1

Bindings use `exact_bound`, `deterministically_reconstructed`,
`probable_non_authoritative`, `unbound`, or `rejected`. Exact bindings require
locatable direct identity evidence. Deterministic bindings require a versioned
algorithm, exact content evidence, the complete candidate set, and a one-to-one
uniqueness proof. Probable and unbound records are never authoritative or
eligible for formal replay.
