# Effective Projection Interpretation v1

B4.8 implements only conservative provisional source interpretation, preserving
B4.6 D1–D5 and D6 Option A. It adds no scientific acceptance threshold, clinical
ontology, measurement category, biological identity or issuer. Original V3/Core
and raw legacy projection fields remain immutable evidence, not effective authority.

Independently represent source-bound study design, factor/grouping semantics,
reported association/outcome direction, reported statistical significance, effect
magnitude/uncertainty, null/absence-of-detection wording, equivalence evidence,
causal-direction authority and formal admission authority. Consume explicit pinned
source annotations with typed dimensions and exact evidence. No universal text
classifier, numerical P threshold or automatic experimental-type inference.

An interventions array is not a manipulation. Unknown enums remain unknown.
Observational biomarker grouping is not assigned treatment. Source-reported
positive/negative associations are reviewable associations, never causal increases
or decreases. A P value alone establishes neither direction nor significance under
this adapter. Preserve the authors' explicitly cited statistical wording/methods
as provisional source assertions. Missing/ambiguous/conflicting evidence abstains.
Nonsignificance, lexical neutral and absence-of-association language do not prove
zero effect, equality, equivalence, noninferiority or an opposite direction.

Original zero signs, increases/decreases strings and layer labels are retained
and marked legacy/non-authoritative. Effective causal sign and zero-effect assertion
are absent; magnitude/HR/CI is not synthesized from a P value. Explicit equivalence
language, if supplied, is preserved as a claim requiring a separate NOT_APPROVED
scientific evaluation contract; no equivalence decision or new threshold is issued.

Versioned sidecars bind raw projection contents, native V3/Core revisions, original
source representations/evidence, pinned provisional annotation, policy/owner scope
and implementation identities. Missing, altered, stale, mismatched, ambiguous or
unsupported inputs fail closed for downstream authority. No sidecar is independent
scientific validation, Human Gold, Strict Core, machine-reuse issuance or formal L4.

Graph construction and effect/causal contradiction/hypothesis consumers must not
use legacy signs/relations as effective authority. Existing Core V2/readiness and
materializer calculations are unchanged; separate semantic guards can only reject
or abstain, never grant permission. B4 source-packet propositions retain their exact
source inputs and are interpreted independently from native records, with no mapping
by count or order. Existing HIF source-bound consistent-findings comparisons remain
reviewable; observational associations cannot silently become opposing causal
effects. Dataset and Atlas expose raw evidence, effective interpretation and guard
reasons separately. Historical evidence remains available even when admission fails.

Derived identities include actual interpretation inputs, policy and implementation
hashes. Exact unchanged raw provider requests may reuse settled cache; new billing,
model extraction and literature acquisition are not authorized by this contract.
