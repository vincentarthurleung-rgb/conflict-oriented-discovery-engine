# Deterministic initial direction discovery v2

Owner scope: `runs/20261031_code_scoped_statistics_direction_preservation_fix_offline/owner_instruction.original.txt`.
This is a derived reporting revision of v1, preserving historical outputs, native
schemas, scientific gates, formal L4 authority and the existing A/B definitions.

An explicit binary extracted direction is preserved independently of statistical
significance. Negation/unknown do not supply a binary direction. Statistical state
is SIGNIFICANT, NONSIGNIFICANT or UNKNOWN; none asserts a zero effect or magnitude.
Eligible conflict-screen votes remain separate from preserved effective signs.
Scoped nonsignificance cannot supply a screening vote. UNKNOWN is not an assertion
of significance and does not by itself suppress an explicit direction.

Statistical statements must come from selected result evidence spans with verified
source identity, text hash and coordinates. Exact endpoint and distinct tested
and reference arms must occur in the same bounded statement. A caption additionally
requires a uniquely selected matching panel. Generic NS glossaries, other panels,
unbound/multiple contrasts or conflicting scoped statuses yield UNKNOWN. No aliases,
P values, thresholds or missing statistical results are inferred. This conservative
matcher may abstain on otherwise understandable source language. The reused Core
comparator-scope audit is structural and confers no statistical or scientific authority.
All accepted and ignored statistical statements retain span/anchor identity,
source-local coordinates and native revision hash.

Legacy tuples lack these scope bindings. Their statistical status remains UNKNOWN;
unscoped NS wording is explicitly labeled as such and retains the preexisting
conservative screening exclusion without deleting their explicit direction.

Preliminary grouping now also partitions exact primary intervention subject/agent,
operator, dose and condition. Missing subjects/operators cannot align relations.
It does not broaden v1 groups or declare scientific equivalence. Distinct factor
relations sharing an endpoint can have source-opposition diagnostics, never A/B
collisions merely from that shared endpoint. Original contexts and uncertainties
remain per observation; associations remain separate from intervention effects.

Observation fields distinguish raw extraction, source direction, effective direction
with derivation, scoped statistics, comparator orientation and screening eligibility.
In v1 pair.raw_signs incorrectly contained effective signs. V2 removes that
ambiguous field and provides raw_extracted_signs, source_reported_signs,
effective_signs and screening_signs. oriented_signs are orientation-adjusted
screening signs. Historical v1 remains audit-only and is not rewritten or silently
reinterpreted. Consumers must read schema_version and explicit v2 fields.

The existing Pilot/Projection callers emit this v2 sidecar and retain its full
contents in Dataset/Atlas. Effective dependency hashes include the implementation,
scope helper and this contract; derived completion identity changes. Exact provider
requests, raw response caches and their independent identities remain unchanged.
No Work comparison or model judgment contributes to A/B. C/D remain uninvoked and
zero in this exploratory lane; no qualification or scientific authority is granted.
