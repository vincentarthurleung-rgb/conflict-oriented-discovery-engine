# Experimental Annotation Targets v1

Comparator, Factor application, and Method tasks carry their Source Envelope, evidence references, candidate answers, ambiguity, allowed labels, and an abstain option. Candidate answers are non-authoritative. Tasks do not ask about Conflict, Comparability, Divergence Explanation, Formal status, or hypotheses and are not executed by this run.

Gold eligibility only selects cases for future independent annotation and adjudication; it never declares Human Gold.
