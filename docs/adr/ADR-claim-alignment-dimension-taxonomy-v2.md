# ADR: Claim Alignment Dimension Taxonomy v2

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Separate proposition identity, observed result, context, and granularity qualification before alignment. Alignment becomes a pair-level record and consumes only proposition cores plus explicit granularity bridge assessments. Contradiction analysis consumes result views and a bound alignment identity.

## Consequences

Result direction no longer prevents two observations from aligning. Context differences remain available downstream but are not proposition identity. Legacy v1 records and candidates remain immutable and separately identifiable.
